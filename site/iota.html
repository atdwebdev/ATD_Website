WE ARE IOTA

Iota’s meaning, “a small amount,” drives our theme.


We draw inspiration from the smallest building blocks of design — pixels. Just as each pixel contributes to a larger, cohesive whole, every member of the Iota class came together to create something greater than ourselves.


Our visual identity extends this idea into space, where Earth is just a speck among countless stars. Just like how Iota also means the ninth star in a constellation, we are the ninth star in the constellation of ATD classes. Even within this vastness, every pixel, every star, every person, matters.


Though Iota represents something small, together, we show that even the smallest elements can leave a cosmic impact.
