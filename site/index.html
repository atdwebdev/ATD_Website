Where We’ve Been

About Us

Alpha Theta Delta is the world's first professional design fraternity, originally founded on May 6th, 2018 at the University of Washington, Seattle.

The Michigan colony (the second ATD colony to exist) was founded on April 24th, 2021 in Ann Arbor, Michigan amidst the COVID-19 pandemic. Our brotherhood has overcome adversity since the beginning, and through design, we hope to solve difficult challenges that the future brings.
