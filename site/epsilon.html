Project Brief

Epsilon Class' visual identity is inspired by jazz, nature, furniture, halftones, and each other. It seamlessly blends elements that convey natural flow with captivating highlights. We create a sensory experience through texture while offering a unique fusion of digital and organic aesthetics.
